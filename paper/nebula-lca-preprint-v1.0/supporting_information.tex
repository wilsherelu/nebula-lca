\section{S1. Scope of this Supporting
Information}\label{s1.-scope-of-this-supporting-information}

This document records the evidence boundaries behind the preprint. It is
intended to accompany the main manuscript on Zenodo. It does not enlarge
the claims made in the paper. The benchmark quantities are
refinery-inspired and hypothetical rather than measured plant data. The
reference audit checks that the cited work or official specification
exists and that the bibliographic metadata used in the manuscript is
supportable; it is not a new systematic literature review.

\section{S2. Authorship and AI-assisted
preparation}\label{s2.-authorship-and-ai-assisted-preparation}

Jiaqi Lu is the sole author and corresponding author. OpenAI language
models, including ChatGPT and Codex, assisted, under the author's
direction, with manuscript restructuring, language editing,
reference-metadata checking, code-to-equation traceability review, and
PDF layout. The human author reviewed the sources, software evidence,
calculations, and final text and accepts responsibility for the work.
The AI systems are not listed as authors because they cannot approve the
final manuscript, hold rights, disclose conflicts, or assume
accountability.

This treatment follows current authorship guidance from COPE and ICMJE
and the Zenodo creator model, which expects a person or organization in
the citable creator field. Historical papers and preprints have placed
systems such as ChatGPT in a byline, but these are treated here as
precedents to disclose, not as a sufficient basis for assigning
authorship responsibility.

\section{S4. Equation-to-code
traceability}\label{s4.-equation-to-code-traceability}

The correspondence below is intentionally module-level rather than tied
to brittle line numbers. Here, PTS means \emph{partially terminated
system}: an aggregated representation that retains selected intermediate
technosphere exchanges for further modeling. The evidence path is the
provider v1 calculation used for the benchmark, the PTS compiler, and a
separate NumPy validation script that reads frozen case artifacts
without importing the production solver. Legacy helpers not exercised by
the five cases are not treated as evidence for the reported results.

\subsection{S5.1. Independent allocation
audit}\label{s5.1.-independent-allocation-audit}

The Case 1 oracle reconstructed every classified source exchange as

\[
\widehat{\mathbf e}_j
=
\sum_{k\in K_j}q_{jk}\widetilde{\mathbf e}_{jk}.
\]

The maximum component error and vector infinity-norm error were both
\(1.39\times10^{-17}\). The relative error was \(1.73\times10^{-16}\). A
negative control that duplicated a product-specific exchange across
product columns produced a nonzero residual and was rejected.

\subsection{S5.2. Independent PTS
oracle}\label{s5.2.-independent-pts-oracle}

For the tested hydrotreating-reforming subsystem, the frozen
coefficients yield

\[
A_{\mathrm{pts}}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1\\
0.2 & 0
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
M=I-A_{\mathrm{pts}}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1\\
-0.2 & 1
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

For a unit reformate demand, the independent solution is

\[
\mathbf x_{\mathrm{pts}}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1.25\\
1.25
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\kappa_1(M)=5.0,
\qquad
\rho_M=0.
\]

Four nonzero declared boundary or elementary-flow components were
compared by exact flow identity, direction, and unit. Every absolute and
relative difference was zero. The expanded subsystem also produced
\(0.0375\) kg of a hydrogen by-product that the compiled shell does not
expose. It is disclosed as a non-exposed technosphere row and is not
silently counted as boundary agreement.

\subsection{S5.3. Identity-indexed EF 3.1
comparison}\label{s5.3.-identity-indexed-ef-3.1-comparison}

The pinned implementation returned 25 ordered indicator records for both
the independently expanded and PTS-integrated calculations. These
records are the outputs of one method set and should not be interpreted
as 25 statistically independent validation dimensions. Only the
climate-change indicator was nonzero for the sparse benchmark inventory:
both calculations returned \(0.1333333333\) kg CO2-Eq, while the other
24 indicator values were zero.

The frozen legacy response attached display metadata by runtime
position, whereas the climate-change and acidification rows were ordered
differently from the static method metadata table. The public
\texttt{data/ef31\_indicator\_comparison.csv} therefore resolves names
and units by canonical indicator identity against
\texttt{nebula-lca-api/data/EF3.1/indicator\_index.csv}; it leaves every
numeric result unchanged. The raw API response remains in the benchmark
package for auditability. The comparison supports propagation and
expanded-compiled equivalence for the tested demand, not independent
validation of EF characterization factors or a complete environmental
profile.
